Assignment questions

Use the case narrative and the separate analytical pages. A strong answer should connect calculations with governance, mission and implementation.

  1. Which operations strategy concept is most appropriate for SARTHAK, and how should it be operationalised at ground level?
  2. Design an operations strategy framework that balances efficiency, demand responsiveness and women’s economic empowerment. Explain its circular feedback logic.
  3. Diagnose the historical forecasting problem using the A/F ratios. How should the initial Tote Bag V2 forecast of 25 units be corrected?
  4. Using the corrected demand model, calculate expected lost sales, expected sales and expected leftovers for alternative production quantities.
  5. Compare the five pricing and clearance strategies. Recommend a production quantity and explain the economic logic.
  6. Should SADRAG consolidate, build local capacity, or use a selective hybrid? State what should be centralised and what should remain local.
  7. How could the recommended intervention be piloted, monitored and revised over subsequent selling cycles?
  8. Compare the strategy with consolidation in a profit-oriented firm. Which practices transfer, and which must be redesigned for social-impact objectives?
