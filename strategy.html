Circular operations strategy

From institutional pressure to learning and renewed innovation

The framework is circular: each selling cycle generates operational outcomes and learning that reshape the next planning cycle.

Consolidation

  • Pool demand and materials
  • Standardise planning rules
  • Coordinate procurement and reporting
  • Risk: weaker local responsiveness

Local capacity building

  • Strengthen demand sensing
  • Test small batches
  • Use local customer feedback
  • Risk: duplication and slower capability development

Selective hybrid

  • Central data and forecast template
  • Shared procurement and inventory visibility
  • Local product experiments and customer learning
  • Regular review and adaptation cycles
Strategic interpretation: centralise the operating backbone, not the entire decision process. Preserve local sensing and women’s participation while standardising the data, planning and clearance mechanisms that protect livelihood outcomes.
