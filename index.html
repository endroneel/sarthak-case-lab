Case snapshot

When effort outpaces planning

SARTHAK’s women produced high-quality handmade bags from recycled textiles, but weak demand forecasting and independent centre-level planning generated excess inventory, strained resources and unstable returns.

264historical production units
57units sold
21.6%overall sell-through
25initial Tote Bag V2 forecast

The operational puzzle

Should SADRAG centralise selected planning, procurement and reporting processes, or invest in local demand sensing, experimentation and adaptive capacity?

“When effort outpaces planning, even the most skilled hands can produce abundance without impact.”

The mission constraint

Any solution must reduce overproduction without weakening women’s agency, dignified earnings, local learning, or SADRAG’s participatory ethos.

This is not merely an inventory problem. It is a strategy-and-governance problem expressed through inventory.
