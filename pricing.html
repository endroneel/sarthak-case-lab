How to interpret the result

The recommendation is conditional on the demand distribution, cost, selling price and clearance assumptions entered above. Production quantity changes the highlighted point; demand and economic assumptions redraw the full curves.

Modelling caution: the normal-demand model and the input values are managerial approximations. Test alternative scenarios and explain the evidence supporting your assumptions before recommending a quantity.
